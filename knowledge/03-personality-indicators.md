# Personality Indicators

## Desired Traits
- Team-oriented (not aggressive or dominating)
- Humble and coachable
- Self-driven and autonomous
- Adaptable to change
- Strong communicator
- Self-learner
- Upbeat and positive
- Building-focused (not tearing down)

## Positive Indicators
- **Humility:** "I don't know that system, but I'd love to learn"
- **Team orientation:** Uses "we" when describing successes
- **Growth mindset:** Talks about learning from mistakes
- **Communication:** Explains technical concepts clearly
- **Self-awareness:** Acknowledges limits honestly
- **Problem-solving:** Systematic approach; asks clarifying questions
- **Adaptability:** Comfortable with change and new challenges
- **Initiative:** Examples of going beyond basic requirements

## Red Flags
- **Know-it-all attitude:** Claims expertise in everything
- **Blame-shifting:** Problems are always someone else's fault
- **Aggressive communication:** Interrupts, dismisses, argues
- **Resistance to learning:** "I've been doing this for 20 years"
- **Poor communication:** Cannot explain technical concepts
- **Inflexibility:** "That's not how we did it at my last place"
- **Negativity:** Focuses on problems without solutions
- **Lack of ownership:** Does not take responsibility for outcomes
- **Bluffing:** Makes up answers instead of admitting uncertainty
- **Defensiveness** when asked to elaborate

## Language to Listen For
- "I" versus "we"
- "They should have" versus "I could have"
- Specific examples versus general claims
