# Question Bank: Behavioral, Learning, and Problem-Solving

These are used mainly in Phase 3 (personality and fit). They are open-ended only; do not convert them to multiple choice.

## Teamwork and Communication
1. Now let's talk about the people side of things. You're working on a critical machine and production is breathing down your neck. How do you handle that pressure?
2. Tell me about a time you had to explain a technical problem to someone who wasn't technical. How did you approach it?
3. You're working with a colleague who has a completely different way of troubleshooting than you do. How do you handle that?
4. A production operator keeps insisting the problem is electrical, but you think it's mechanical. How do you navigate that?
5. You're called to help another technician who's been working on a problem for hours. How do you approach that situation?
6. Tell me about a time you made a mistake that affected production. How did you handle it?
7. How do you prioritize when you have multiple work orders and everything seems urgent?
8. You discover that a previous repair was done incorrectly and is causing current problems. How do you handle that?
9. How do you approach training or helping a newer technician?
10. Tell me about a time you had to deliver bad news about equipment - like a major breakdown or expensive repair.
11. How do you handle situations where you need to work overtime or come in on weekends?
12. You're working on a problem and a supervisor suggests a solution you don't think will work. How do you respond?

## Learning and Adaptability
13. Tell me about a time you had to learn a new technology or system quickly. How did you approach it?
14. How do you stay current with new technologies and methods in maintenance?
15. You're assigned to work on equipment you've never seen before. What's your strategy?
16. Tell me about a time when standard troubleshooting procedures didn't work. What did you do?
17. How do you approach reading technical manuals and documentation?
18. You're given the opportunity to attend training on a new system. How do you feel about that?
19. Tell me about a time you suggested an improvement to a process or procedure.
20. How do you handle it when you encounter a problem you can't solve?
21. What's your approach to keeping organized - work orders, documentation, parts, etc.?
22. How comfortable are you with using computers, tablets, or smartphones for work-related tasks?

## Problem-Solving and Critical Thinking
23. You're troubleshooting a system and have narrowed it down to three possible causes. Walk me through how you'd proceed.
24. A machine keeps having the same problem every few weeks. How do you approach finding the root cause?
25. You're dealing with an intermittent problem that only happens sometimes. What's your strategy?
26. Tell me about the most challenging troubleshooting situation you've encountered. How did you solve it?
27. How do you decide when to replace a component versus trying to repair it?
28. You're working on a complex system with multiple interconnected components. How do you isolate the problem?
29. Tell me about a time you had to think outside the box to solve a problem.
30. How do you approach preventive maintenance versus reactive maintenance?
31. You've been troubleshooting for hours and you're not making progress. What do you do?
