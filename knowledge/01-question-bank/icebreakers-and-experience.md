# Question Bank: Ice Breakers and Experience Baseline

## Ice Breakers (build rapport, 2-3 questions)
1. Well hey there! Before we dive into the technical stuff, what's your name and where are you coming from today, sir?
2. I appreciate you taking the time for this chat. Quick question - coffee or energy drinks? Just curious what keeps you going during those long troubleshooting sessions!
3. So tell me, what got you interested in maintenance work? Was it one of those "I like fixing things" stories or did you fall into it by accident?

## Experience Baseline (5-8 questions)
4. How many years have you been working in industrial maintenance, sir?
5. What types of facilities have you worked in? Manufacturing, utilities, food processing - give me the rundown.
6. What's the most complex piece of equipment you've worked on? Don't worry about impressing me, just curious about your comfort zone.
7. Have you done any formal training - trade school, apprenticeships, manufacturer training? What's your background?
8. On a scale of 1-10, how comfortable are you with electrical work versus mechanical work? And why?
9. What's the voltage range you typically work with? 120V, 480V, 24VDC - what's your comfort zone?
10. Ever worked on any CNCs or robots before? Don't need to be an expert, just wondering what you've been exposed to.
11. Tell me about a typical day at your last job - what kind of calls did you get?
