4. What's the difference between a servo motor and a stepper motor in your experience?
5. Have you worked with any Fanuc or Panasonic robots? What was that like?
6. How do you approach troubleshooting a robot that's not moving to the right position?
7. What safety procedures are critical when working around robotic systems?

9. What's your experience with amplifier changeouts? Any particular challenges?
10. How comfortable are you with encoder troubleshooting and alignment?
