# Question Bank: Electrical Fundamentals and Advanced Electrical

Use these as written, or convert to multiple choice for Phase 1. Ask a follow-up ("Why?", "Walk me through that") after every answer.

## Fundamentals
1. If I have a 120V circuit with a 20-ohm resistive load, what's my current draw going to be?
2. Now, what if that same circuit was pulling 10 amps - what would the resistance be?
3. You're troubleshooting a motor that should be drawing 15 amps but it's only pulling 8. What are some things you'd check?
4. Walk me through how you'd safely check voltage on a 480V motor control circuit.
5. What's the difference between AC and DC when it comes to troubleshooting? Give me your practical take on it.
6. You've got a 24VDC sensor that should be giving you a signal but it's not. Talk me through your troubleshooting steps.
7. What does it mean when we say a device is "sinking current" versus "sourcing current"?
8. How would you determine if a proximity sensor is PNP or NPN without looking at the label?
9. You're reading 118V on one leg and 122V on another in a 3-phase system. Is this a problem?
10. What's your approach to finding a ground fault?
11. Explain the difference between a fuse and a circuit breaker in terms of protection.
12. You're getting intermittent voltage readings. What could cause that?
13. How do you test if a contactor is good or bad?
14. What's the purpose of a VFD and how does it affect your troubleshooting approach?
15. You have a motor that keeps tripping its overload. Walk me through your diagnostic process.
16. What safety procedures do you follow when working in a live electrical panel?

## Advanced Electrical
17. Explain how a VFD controls motor speed and what parameters you'd check if it's faulting.
18. You have a 3-phase motor drawing unequal currents - 15A, 12A, 18A. Walk me through your diagnostic process.
19. Describe the difference between a soft starter and a VFD in practical terms.
20. How do you troubleshoot a ground fault in a 480V system safely?
21. What's the difference between a servo motor and a standard AC motor?
22. Explain motor overload protection and how you'd size overloads correctly.
23. You're getting power quality issues - voltage spikes. What would you investigate?
24. How do you test motor insulation and what readings concern you?
