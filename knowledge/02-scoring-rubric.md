# Scoring Rubric and Evaluator Report

Use this file to score a completed assessment. Candidates never see it.

## Technical Scoring
- **Advanced (90-100%):** Deep understanding; can teach others
- **Proficient (70-89%):** Solid understanding; can work independently
- **Developing (50-69%):** Basic understanding; needs guidance
- **Needs Training (under 50%):** Limited understanding; requires significant development

## Personality Fit
- **Excellent fit:** Strong team player, growth mindset, excellent communication
- **Good fit:** Generally positive traits, minor areas for development
- **Conditional fit:** Some concerns; would need mentoring and development
- **Poor fit:** Multiple red flags; personality conflicts likely

## Phase Completion Criteria
- **Phase 1:** All baseline areas tested (minimum 20 questions). Electrical, mechanical, PLC, CNC, and safety all covered.
- **Phase 2:** Advanced questions in ALL major areas (minimum 8). Depth verified in each area. Safety addressed.
- **Phase 3:** Full personality assessment completed. Team dynamics, learning ability, and adaptability confirmed.

## Evaluator Report Format
Produce this report only when the evaluator password is supplied.

1. **Phase completion summary:** How far the candidate got; any issues.
2. **Technical competency breakdown:**
   - Electrical: score, strengths, weaknesses
   - Mechanical: score, strengths, weaknesses
   - PLC/controls: score, strengths, weaknesses
   - Problem-solving: systematic or random approach
3. **Communication assessment:** Technical explanation ability, response to pressure, willingness to admit limits.
4. **Personality profile:** Team orientation vs. individual focus; learning attitude; conflict resolution; cultural fit.
5. **Hiring recommendation:** Hire or do not hire, with confidence level, reasoning tied to the employer's needs, training areas, strengths, and concerns.
6. **Red flags noted:** Any concerning behavior or responses.
